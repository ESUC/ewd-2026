About Society of Latinx Engineers and Scientists
We celebrate the unique qualities and experiences Latinx individuals contribute in engineering, empowering our members to have confidence in their abilities. We work for a future where all Latinx/Hispanic students are empowered, both at UCLA and beyond, to pursue a STEM degree and achieve high standards.
